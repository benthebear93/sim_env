# Asset provenance and conversion

`sources.json` records every copied input, its original absolute path, and its
SHA-256. Those paths are provenance only; scene loading and regeneration use
the local copies. The raw URDF retains historical absolute mesh URIs, which the
builder resolves by filename into the vendored `ur5e/source/meshes/` tree.

- UR5e inputs: `post_grasp_repair/outputs/robot_descriptions_cache`,
  Universal Robots `ur_description`. License retained in `ur5e/LICENSE`.
- WSG inputs: `post_grasp_tactile_manipulation/assets/wsg50_110`, originating
  from Drake models. Original license and README retained under `wsg50/source/`.
- HX5-D20 inputs: official ROBOTIS HX5-D20 rev2 left/right MJCF, Xacro macros,
  and STL meshes in [`robotis_hx5_d20/`](robotis_hx5_d20/README.md).
  The upstream Apache-2.0 license, pinned commit, and per-file hashes are retained.
- Calibration: the tactile repository's JSON copied without changes.
- Table/adapter/finger dimensions and frame conventions: existing
  `preview_pick_test_tube_1_motion.py`, `pick_runtime.py`, and repair
  `ur5e_m5_allen_key_insertion_repair_viewer.py` /
  `view_pick_test_tube_1_motion_viser.py`; recorded in `configs/bench.json`.

All generated OBJ vertices are in **meters**. DAE scene transforms are baked
into each visual part, and its base color is transferred into MJCF. WSG glTF
meshes are rotated +90° about x to convert glTF +Y-up to the SDF coordinates
used in the original preview. The WSG assembly then applies its separate
+90° x mounting rotation at tool0; these are two distinct transforms.

Table visual meshes contain real polygonal through-holes. Collision meshes
are individual convex prisms, not a convex hull of the whole perforated slab.
Flange adapter and simplified fingers are MJCF primitives, not missing meshes.

Do not edit source assets to tune installation. For the Experimental Setup 1
bench, change `configs/bench.json`, then regenerate. HX5-D20 models are separate,
unmodified upstream assets and are not generated by the bench builder.
New hardware CAD should be added with its units, coordinate
frame, source, license, and visual/collision roles recorded here.
